## 2026-07-17 — Kit v1

### Signup facts (the no-bank-info concern)

- Creating a seller account + posting gigs requires **email + phone verification only** — no bank or card info. ([Fiverr Help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050063113-How-do-I-create-a-Fiverr-account))
- Payment method is only needed to **withdraw earnings**, and PayPal / Payoneer / Fiverr Revenue Card all work — bank transfer is just one option. Decide after the first sale. ([Fiverr Help: payments](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050216753-Payment-methods), [Wise guide](https://wise.com/us/blog/how-to-get-paid-on-fiverr))

### Gig 1 — Resume rewrite (main gig)

- **Title:** I will rewrite your resume to get past ATS and land interviews
- **Category:** Writing & Translation → Resume Writing. **Tags:** resume writing, ats resume, resume rewrite, job application, career
- **Packages:**
  - Basic $25 — full rewrite of an existing resume, 1 page, 48h, 1 revision
  - Standard $50 — rewrite tailored to a specific job posting + keyword optimization, 48h, 2 revisions
  - Premium $85 — tailored rewrite + cover letter + LinkedIn About section, 72h, 3 revisions
- **Description:**
  > Your resume gets ~7 seconds with a recruiter — if it even gets past the ATS software first. I rewrite it to survive both.
  >
  > What you get: a complete rewrite (not a template dump) — achievement-focused bullet points with real metrics, keywords matched to your target role so applicant tracking systems rank you higher, clean formatting recruiters can scan, and zero fluff.
  >
  > How it works: send your current resume and the kind of job you're targeting (a specific posting is even better). You get your rewrite within 48 hours, with revisions until it feels right.
  >
  > I use modern AI-assisted drafting combined with human judgment on every order — which means faster turnaround and sharper tailoring than either alone. Every document is written fresh for you.
- **Buyer requirements:** (1) current resume (any format, even messy notes), (2) target job title/industry — paste a job posting if you have one, (3) anything you're proud of that isn't on the resume yet.
- **FAQ:** "Is this AI-generated?" → "AI-assisted, human-finished. Every order is individually tailored and reviewed — never auto-generated output." / "No resume at all?" → "Order Basic + message me; I'll build from a questionnaire."

### Gig 2 — Cover letter + LinkedIn (upsell gig)

- **Packages:** Basic $15 — 1 tailored cover letter, 24h / Standard $30 — cover letter + LinkedIn About rewrite / Premium $45 — 3 cover letter variants for 3 different postings
- **Description:**
